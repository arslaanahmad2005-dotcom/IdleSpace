📌 Overview

IdleSpace is an AI-powered marketplace that connects businesses with unused commercial spaces to individuals and organizations looking for affordable short-term rentals.

Many commercial spaces such as restaurants, schools, hotels, and offices remain unused for several hours every day. IdleSpace enables business owners to monetize these idle assets while providing startups, creators, trainers, and event organizers with flexible hourly booking options.

---

## ❗ Problem Statement

Every day, businesses lose potential revenue because valuable spaces remain unused.

Examples include:
- 🍽️ Restaurant kitchens during off-peak hours
- 🏫 School classrooms after school hours
- 🏨 Hotel conference rooms
- 🏢 Office meeting rooms

At the same time, many users struggle to find affordable, verified spaces for short-term use.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

IdleSpace provides a centralized platform where businesses can list their unused spaces and customers can discover, book, and pay for them on an hourly basis.

The platform leverages AI to improve search results, recommend suitable spaces, optimize pricing, and enhance the overall booking experience.
